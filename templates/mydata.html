🤔 What can a website do with your IP address?

  • 📍 Approximate location — city, region and country, without GPS
  • 🏠 Find your home — your home IP rarely changes; over time it can be linked to your address
  • 🕐 Track you across websites — even without cookies, the same IP appearing on different sites links your activity
  • 👤 Link family members — everyone at home shares the same IP, so your sibling's account can be connected to yours
  • 🚫 Block or ban you — platforms can ban your IP, not just your account
  • 📡 Identify your internet provider — which can be legally forced to reveal your name and address

A VPN hides your real IP — but most school networks block VPNs. And the website still knows someone at that VPN server visited.
